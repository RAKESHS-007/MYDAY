            Boxing training

            Future

            Boxing will sit beside your strength plan. Start after the B.Ed training ends, or sooner if your schedule allows. Learn the basics from a coach so your technique stays safe.

            Phase 1: Foundations

            Stance, guard, jab, cross, footwork and shadow boxing, 3 rounds of 3 minutes. Your skipping rope and gripper already help here.

            Phase 2: Bag work

            Heavy bag combinations, hooks and body shots, 6 rounds of 3 minutes with 1 minute rest. Keep your two strength days.

            Phase 3: Pads and conditioning

            Pad work with a coach, defence and light technical sparring. Add it only when your coach says you are ready.
